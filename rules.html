The Court

A standard NBA court is 28 meters long and 15 meters wide. The arched line near the basket is the three-point line — shots from outside it are worth 3 points.

Scoring

  • 3 points — a basket made from beyond the three-point line.
  • 2 points — a regular field goal inside the three-point line.
  • 1 point — a free throw after a foul.

Game Length

A game has four quarters. In the NBA, each quarter is 12 minutes; in FIBA (international rules), each is 10 minutes. If tied at the end, teams play overtime (5 minutes) until there is a winner.

Common Violations

  • Traveling — moving your pivot foot illegally (extra steps).
  • Double dribble — dribbling, stopping, then dribbling again.
  • 24-second shot clock — the offense must shoot within 24 seconds.
  • 3 seconds — an offensive player cannot stay in the paint for more than 3 seconds.

Fouls

A foul is illegal body contact — pushing, holding, or hitting an opponent. A player who reaches 6 fouls (NBA) or 5 fouls (FIBA) fouls out and must leave the game.
