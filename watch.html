How to Watch a Game Like a Fan

Watching basketball is not just about following the ball. Here is what to look for as a beginner.

Pick a Team to Follow

It is much easier to enjoy the game when you have a team to support. For beginners, the Golden State Warriors (fast-paced, great shooting), the Los Angeles Lakers (star power), or your local CBA team are good starting points.

Watch Without the Ball Sometimes

New fans stare at the ball. Experienced fans watch the off-ball movement: who is setting screens, who is cutting to the basket, who is open. That is where the strategy happens.

Etiquette at the Arena

  • Do not wave objects during a free throw.
  • Return to your seat during live play.
  • Respect the referees and the visiting team.

Where to Watch in China

NBA games are usually streamed on Tencent Sports or Migu Video. CBA games are often on CCTV-5 or Migu. Check the current season's broadcast schedule, as rights change every year.

Start with a Playoff Game

If you only watch one game, pick a playoff "Game 7" (winner advances, loser goes home). The intensity is higher, and every possession matters.
